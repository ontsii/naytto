Bart Simpson

Name

Bartholomew Jo-Jo Simpson

Description

Bart Simpson is the mischievous, rebellious son of Homer and Marge Simpson. He is known for his catchphrase "Eat my shorts!" and his love of skateboarding and pranks. Despite his troublemaking ways, Bart has a good heart and often shows loyalty to his family and friends.
